Browser Strategy Game

Viking Chess

A playable Hnefatafl-inspired tafl game for two local players. Defenders escort the king to a corner. Attackers surround the king and shut down the escape.

How to Play

  • Pieces move orthogonally any number of open squares.
  • Capture by trapping an enemy between two hostile sides.
  • The king wins by reaching any glowing corner refuge.
  • Attackers win by surrounding the king on all four sides.
