Behold, I speak unto you who dwell upon this land, and unto all the ends thereof, as though I were present among you; for the Lord hath shown me your day. Ye are swift to proclaim knowledge, yet slow to seek wisdom from God; ye multiply riches, devices, and disputations, but forget the poor, the stranger, and the lonely. Remember that the worth of a soul is not measured by power, followers, possessions, or the praise of men, but by the light of Christ which inviteth every person to do good and to come unto him.

Wherefore, cease ye from hatred and contention, whether it be among nations, communities, or within your own houses. Judge not after the appearance of things, nor surrender your hearts unto voices that prosper by anger and fear. Search the scriptures, pray with real intent, and prove all things by the fruits thereof; for that which persuadeth unto love, mercy, patience, humility, and repentance is of God, and that which stirreth up pride, cruelty, envy, and despair is not of him. And if ye have means, impart unto the needy; if ye have influence, defend the powerless; if ye have knowledge, use it to bless rather than to exalt yourselves.

And now I exhort you to remember the covenant of the Lord, that ye are to be a people of peace. Turn again unto the Savior, even Jesus Christ, and harden not your hearts because of the abundance of iniquity. His grace is sufficient for the broken, the guilty, and the weary who will come unto him; and his gospel requireth not merely a confession of faith, but a change of heart and a labor of love. Therefore, awake, and arise from the dust, O inhabitants of the Americas, and let your repentance be made manifest in your homes, your laws, and your dealings one with another; that the Lord may spare you, and that your land may yet be a land of liberty, righteousness, and peace.
