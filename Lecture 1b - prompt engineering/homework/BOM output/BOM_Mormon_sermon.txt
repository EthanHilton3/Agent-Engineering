Behold, I speak unto you who dwell upon the lands of the Americas, and unto all the ends of the earth who shall hear these words. Ye have been blessed with knowledge, abundance, and power to communicate one with another; nevertheless, many hearts are lifted up in pride, and contention hath become as a consuming fire among you. Ye seek after riches, praise, and the wisdom of the world, and forget the God who hath given you breath and brought you forth from the dust. Remember that the worth of every soul is great in the sight of God, and that no nation, party, tribe, or people is made righteous by its name, but by its repentance, humility, and works of mercy.

Wherefore, I beseech you to cease from hatred and from delighting in the destruction of one another. Judge not according to the appearance of things, neither spread words that kindle strife, for every idle word and every secret work shall be brought to light. Defend the weak, succor the poor, mourn with those that mourn, and refuse to make merchandise of the fears and miseries of your fellow beings. Teach your children truth, and let your homes be places of prayer, forgiveness, and patience. For faith without charity profiteth nothing; and charity is the pure love of Christ, which suffereth long, envieth not, seeketh not her own, and rejoiceth in truth.

Therefore, repent, O ye inhabitants of the earth, and harden not your hearts against the voice of the Lord. Turn from violence, dishonesty, lust, and pride, and seek peace in your families and communities. Trust not in the arm of flesh, nor in the changing praises of men, but build upon the rock of the Redeemer, who is Jesus Christ. If ye will come unto him with a broken heart and a contrite spirit, he will heal your divisions, strengthen your weakness, and make of you one people; and then shall your lands be blessed, not because of your greatness, but because ye remember the God of the land and keep his commandments.
