Behold, I speak unto you who dwell in the lands of the Americas, as though ye were present, and I speak unto you as if ye had ears to hear. The days are full of contention, fear, and the seeking of riches; and many hearts are lifted up because of knowledge, power, and the praise of men. Yet remember that all things which are good cometh from God, and that no device of men can bring peace unto the soul. Wherefore, lay aside hatred, envy, and the desire to be esteemed above your neighbor, and seek ye first to succor the poor, to comfort those that mourn, and to be witnesses of God at all times and in all places, even until the end of your lives.

And now I exhort you to search the word of God, not that ye may strive one with another, but that ye may know the voice of the Good Shepherd. Pray unto the Father with a sincere heart, and ask him if these things are not true; and if ye ask with a sincere heart, with real intent, having faith in Christ, he will manifest the truth of it unto you by the power of the Holy Ghost. Trust not in the arm of flesh, nor in the loudness of the multitude, nor in the cunning words of those who would turn your hearts from righteousness; for the wisdom of God is foolishness unto the world, and the meek shall inherit that which the proud cannot purchase.

Wherefore, have charity, which is the pure love of Christ, and suffer long, and be kind, and seek not your own. Let your homes, your communities, and your nations be established upon repentance, mercy, and truth. And if ye shall come unto Christ, and deny yourselves of all ungodliness, and love God with all your might, mind and strength, then is his grace sufficient for you, and his power shall make weak things strong. I leave you these words as a warning and a blessing: be faithful, be united in righteousness, and fear not, for the Lord remembereth his covenant, and his mercy endureth forever.
