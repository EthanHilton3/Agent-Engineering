## Title

In Appreciation of Friction: Embracing the Awkward

## Speaker

C. Shane Reese

## Key Takeaways

- Embrace awkward and uncomfortable moments as essential opportunities for personal growth and development.
- Face social discomfort rather than avoid it, as meaningful connections and service arise from human interactions—not from frictionless, technology-mediated experiences.
- Worship in the house of the Lord and prioritize spiritual development, even amid busy academic life.
- BYU’s traditions, standards, and community are designed to help students become more Christlike.
- Jesus Christ is the ultimate example of reaching out to others, engaging personally, and ministering beyond comfort and convenience.

## Speaker Emphasis

- Repeated encouragement to 'embrace the awkward' as a sign of learning and progress.
- Warning against 'frictionless' living enabled by technology, encouraging active engagement with life’s challenges.
- The value of BYU’s dress and grooming standards as formative rather than restrictive.
- The importance of turning outward—toward Christ and others—instead of inward and downward (toward screens, devices, and isolation).
- Personal stories (his own and others') were used to stress the impact of pushing beyond one’s comfort zone.
