Behold, I, Mormon, write unto you who dwell in the lands which the Lord hath preserved for a wise purpose; and I speak unto you as though ye were present. Ye have waxed strong in knowledge, in riches, and in the works of your hands, yet if ye forget the Giver of these things, your strength shall become a snare unto you. Contend not one with another because of color, tongue, station, or possession; for the devil seeketh to stir up hatred among the children of men, that he may have power over them. Remember that all are alike unto God, and that the worth of every soul is great in his sight; wherefore, succor the poor, defend the weak, welcome the stranger, and judge righteous judgment with humility and mercy.

And now I would speak unto the hearts of individuals: cease to nourish anger in secret, envy not the prosperity of your neighbor, and make not an idol of ease, reputation, or appetite. Pray unto the Father in the name of Jesus Christ, and seek his word amid the multitude of voices that clamor for your loyalty. For behold, faith is not found in the pride of men, nor is truth established by the loudness of contention, but by a broken heart, a contrite spirit, and the fruits of righteousness. If ye have sinned, despair not; repent, and come unto Christ, who is mighty to save and to cleanse the humble.

Therefore, repent, O ye inhabitants of the Americas, and harden not your hearts against the poor, the afflicted, or the voice of the Lord. Build your communities upon covenant, honesty, and service, lest division consume your inheritance. And I say unto you, as one who hath seen the sorrow that followeth pride: if ye will remember Jesus Christ, keep his commandments, and have charity one for another, your lands may yet be a land of peace; but if ye cast these things aside, ye shall learn that no nation, family, or soul can stand long upon injustice. Wherefore, choose ye this day whom ye will serve, and may the grace of Christ abide with all those who seek him in sincerity. Amen.
