Author: Moroni
Sermon Title: An Exhortation to Remember
----------------------------------------

Behold, I speak unto you who dwell upon this land in the latter days, even unto all nations, kindreds, tongues, and people. I know that ye are encompassed about by confusion, contention, and the vain traditions of men; and many seek after riches, power, and the praise of the world, supposing that these things can give peace unto the soul. But I say unto you, remember that ye are dependent upon the mercy of God, and that every good gift cometh from him. Wherefore, if ye will humble yourselves, and repent, and come unto Christ with a broken heart and a contrite spirit, he will make weak things become strong unto you, and he will lead you in paths of peace amid the storms of the earth.

And now I would exhort you to cease striving one against another, and to succor those who stand in need of succor, and to mourn with those that mourn, and to comfort those that stand in need of comfort. Judge not according to the outward appearance, neither despise the poor, the stranger, or him whose voice is different from your own; for the Lord remembereth every nation, and his desire is that ye should love one another as he hath loved you. Let your homes be places of prayer, your words be seasoned with truth, and your dealings be honest, that ye may not add sorrow unto the world by your pride or bitterness.

Wherefore, seek not for signs that ye may believe, but seek wisdom through faith, prayer, and the words of Christ. And if ye shall ask with a sincere heart, having real intent, he will manifest the truth unto you by the power of the Holy Ghost. Be faithful in small things, forgive freely, and endure unto the end; for the night of darkness shall pass away, and the day cometh when every knee shall bow and every tongue confess that Jesus is the Christ. And I bid you farewell, hoping that ye will remember these things, and that the grace of God the Father, and of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the communion of the Holy Ghost, may be with you forever. Amen.
