Behold, I speak unto you who dwell upon the land of promise, and unto all nations that have come hither, even unto the latter days. I perceive that ye are lifted up in the pride of your hearts, trusting in riches, in the wisdom of men, and in the works of your own hands; and behold, contention, fear, and hatred are multiplied among you. Yet the Lord remembereth you, and his arm is stretched forth still. Wherefore, awake, and arise from the dust, O ye inhabitants of the earth, and harden not your hearts against the truth because of the weakness of those who bear it. Search the word of God, and prove all things by the power of the Holy Ghost, for every good thing inviteth and enticeth to do good, to love God, and to serve him.

And now I exhort you to remember that faith is not a shield for cruelty, nor is liberty a license to despise your neighbor. Lift up the hands which hang down, feed the hungry, receive the stranger, forgive those who trespass against you, and speak truth without wrath. Let your homes be places of prayer, your learning be tempered with humility, and your dealings be just, that ye may become a people who mourn with those that mourn and rejoice in righteousness. For charity is the pure love of Christ, and it endureth forever; and whoso is found possessed of it at the last day, it shall be well with him.

Therefore, come unto Christ, and be perfected in him, and deny yourselves of all ungodliness. Take upon you his name, not merely with your lips, but in your works and in the manner of your living. Pray unto the Father with all the energy of your hearts, that ye may be filled with this love, which he hath bestowed upon all who are true followers of his Son. And if ye shall do these things, the darkness of your day shall not overcome you, for the light shineth in darkness; and the Lord shall lead you by the hand until faith is changed to knowledge and peace is established in your souls.
