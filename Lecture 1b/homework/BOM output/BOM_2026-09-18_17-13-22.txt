Behold, I speak unto you who dwell upon the land of the Americas, and unto all them that have received abundance from the hand of the Lord. I perceive that ye have great knowledge, and mighty works, and devices whereby ye may speak one unto another across the earth; nevertheless, many hearts are far from God, and contention, pride, and the love of riches have become as a chain upon the souls of men. Wherefore, remember that the worth of a soul is not measured by possessions, fame, or power, but by the light of Christ which inviteth every man to do good and to come unto him.

And now I would exhort you to seek peace in your homes, in your communities, and among the nations; for behold, the adversary stirreth up anger that he might divide the children of men. Judge not according to the appearance of things, neither delight in the humiliation of the weak, but succor the poor, forgive those who trespass against you, and mourn with those that mourn. Let your words be tempered with truth and charity, and let your works testify that ye are disciples of Jesus Christ; for if ye have not charity, ye are nothing, notwithstanding all your knowledge and labor.

Therefore, humble yourselves before the Lord, and pray with real intent, that ye may know the truth of all things. Search the words of the prophets, but also treasure the whisperings of the Holy Ghost, which leadeth unto virtue, patience, and faith in Christ. And when ye shall receive these things, ask God, in the name of Christ, if they be not true; and if ye shall ask with a sincere heart, he will manifest the truth unto you by the power of the Holy Ghost. And I bid you farewell, hoping that ye will come unto Christ, be perfected in him, and deny not his power, that peace may yet abide upon this choice land.
