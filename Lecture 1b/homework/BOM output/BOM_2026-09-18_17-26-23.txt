Author: Moroni
Sermon Title: An Exhortation to the Nations of the Americas
----------------------------------------

Behold, I speak unto you who dwell in the lands of the Americas, and unto all them who shall read these words in the latter days. I know that ye are encompassed about by mighty works, and by the wisdom of men; nevertheless, many hearts are lifted up, and the poor and the stranger are forgotten. Therefore, remember that the Lord is the giver of all good things, and that no nation can long prosper in iniquity. Turn ye unto Christ, and harden not your hearts because of the abundance of your possessions, neither trust in the arm of flesh, but seek first the kingdom of God, that ye may have peace amid the tumults of the world.

And I would exhort you to remember the worth of every soul before God. Cease to strive one against another because of color, language, station, or opinion; for the devil seeketh to divide, that he may lead you captive. Feed the hungry, clothe the naked, visit the sick and afflicted, and minister unto those who are cast aside. Judge righteously, yet condemn not in pride; speak truth, yet let your words be seasoned with charity. For if ye have not charity, ye are nothing, and the knowledge of all things shall not profit you.

Wherefore, awake, and arise from the dust, O inhabitants of these lands, and come unto Christ, who is the author and finisher of your faith. Pray in your homes, teach your children righteousness, forgive one another, and use the powers given you to preserve liberty, justice, and peace. And by the power of the Holy Ghost ye may know the truth of all things, if ye ask with a sincere heart and real intent. Be faithful unto the end, and may the grace of God the Father, and of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the communion of the Holy Ghost, be with you forever and ever. Amen.
