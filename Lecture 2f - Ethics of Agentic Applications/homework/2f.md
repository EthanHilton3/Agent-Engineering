# Homework 2f: Final Project Initial Thoughts

## What I want to build

I'm building an app called **Continuity**, a media journal you can just talk to. Instead of filling out a form for every movie, show, or book, you ramble about your night to the assistant (the "Lorekeeper"), like: "Jake came over and we watched two more episodes of Severance, then started Dune but fell asleep halfway." It figures out what happened, finds the real titles and episodes, and shows you entries to confirm before anything saves.

The app has two main sections: **Previously** for everything you've watched or read, and **Next Time** for your goals. You can also ask things like "how am I doing on my goals?" or "what would it take to finish this show by December 20th?"

## Why this one

I've wanted a tracker like this for a long time, so I'd actually use it. I also think it's a real fit for an LLM. A form can store the same data, but it can't untangle a messy story with self-corrections, changing dates, and things like "two more episodes."

My main core concept will be **tool calling**, since the agent has to look up titles, check my history, and manage my goals. **Structured output** is the second one, for turning a messy transcript into clean events. Code still does the math and has to save everything, and nothing gets saved until I confirm it.

## What else I considered

- **KeepSaid semantic search:** Adding embeddings to my quote app was my backup. It's a safe idea, but it felt like mostly adding a search bar, and it needs lots of quotes to feel useful.
- **Ultimate tracker with recommendations:** Most of my time would go to regular tracking features, and I wasn't sure recommendations really needed an LLM. Continuity is basically the part of this idea I care about most.
- **A Jev game:** I thought about a bigger version of my guard game with an avatar and a real environment. It would've been a really fun demo, but I decided I wanted something practical instead of a game.
- **Other app ideas:** I also looked at a spoiler blocker, a tool that pulls the best quotes out of group chats (Quote Miner), and a rulebook assistant. Quote Miner is my fallback if Continuity doesn't work out.

I went with an app instead of a research question because I wanted something I could keep using and show off later.
