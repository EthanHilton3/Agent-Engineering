# Homework 3d: Continuity progress

This week I spent more than 3 hours on my final project, **Continuity**, a voice journal for the shows, movies and books I consume. My base app works now but now I'm just working on finetuning it and adding better features. I started by just using the app like a real person, and it worked but didn't feel good yet. A messy memo took over 30 seconds, follow-up messages sometimes started a whole new conversation, and the wording felt like a task tracker. So I worked with Claude on a round of fixes, tested them by hand, and found a few more things to fix after that.

The app now feels a lot more like talking to someone. The assistant is a real chat that remembers the conversation, and I can talk to it and watch my words appear as I speak. It shows what it's doing while it works ("Looking up Severance...") instead of leaving me staring at a blank screen, and my test memo went from about 29 seconds to about 20 while costing a third less. The biggest speedup came from skipping steps that didn't need an AI at all: plain summaries, goal progress cards and schedules are now drawn by regular code, which is faster and means the AI can't make up numbers. It can also build a night-by-night plan for finishing a goal, and I can save that plan and see it tracked against what I've actually watched. The safety checks that try to read or change another user's data all still pass, and my accuracy test on 15 real memos stayed at about 80% with zero invented entries.

The most useful lesson was that tests passing isn't the same as an app feeling good. I only noticed the slowness and the broken follow-ups by using it myself. I also learned that making an AI app faster is mostly about removing steps, and that when I tried making the AI "think less" to save time, it started skipping questions it should ask, so I only keep a speedup if the accuracy holds up on repeated runs. Next I'm fixing a few more things I found while testing (searching for a whole list of movies at once, clearer cards, and one continuous chat history), then measuring accuracy and cost properly and getting it hosted so I can use it on my phone. I'm on track for the deadline.
